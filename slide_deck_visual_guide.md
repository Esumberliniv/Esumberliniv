# Slide Deck Visual Guide

## Slide Design Recommendations
- **Color Scheme**: Use Bleacher Report brand colors (black, white, orange accents)
- **Font**: Clean, modern sans-serif (Arial, Helvetica, or BR brand font)
- **Less is More**: Maximum 6 bullet points per slide
- **Visual Balance**: Use icons and numbers to break up text

## Additional Design Tips:

### Icons to Use:
- 📊 for data/metrics
- ⚡ for speed/automation
- 📱 for app/mobile
- 🎯 for goals/targets
- 💡 for insights/learnings

### Animation Suggestions:
- Slide 2: Reveal each accomplishment one by one
- Slide 4: Animate the before/after transition
- Slide 6: Numbers could count up for effect

### Color Coding:
- **Primary**: Black background, white text
- **Accent**: Orange for emphasis (numbers, key points)
- **Success**: Green for positive metrics
- **Charts**: Use BR brand colors
