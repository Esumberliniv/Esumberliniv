# Speaker Notes & Presentation Guide
## Intern Showcase - Bleacher Report

### Timing Breakdown (7-8 minutes total)
- **Slide 1**: Introduction (20 seconds)
- **Slide 2**: Overview (30 seconds)
- **Slide 3**: Content Expansion (90 seconds)
- **Slide 4**: Automation Tool (2 minutes)
- **Slide 5**: BR App UX (90 seconds)
- **Slide 6**: Results & Impact (60 seconds)
- **Slide 7**: Key Learnings (45 seconds)
- **Slide 8**: Thank You (15 seconds)
- **Q&A**: 2-3 minutes

---

### Slide 1: Title Slide
**Say**: "Good [morning/afternoon], I'm [Your Name], and I'm excited to share three key projects I've completed during my summer internship at Bleacher Report."

**Tip**: Make eye contact, smile, show enthusiasm

---

### Slide 2: Overview
**Say**: "Over the past few months, I've focused on three areas that directly impact both our content pipeline and user experience: expanding our content sources, automating roster management, and improving our app's UX."

**Tip**: Use hand gestures to count off the three items

---

### Slide 3: Content Expansion
**Say**: "When I started in June, we were adding about 30 Twitter sources per week. I recognized an opportunity, especially with the rise of sports podcasts. By strategically targeting high-value sources like LeBron's podcast and local beat reporters, I've added over 220 new sources - that's a 7x improvement in our sourcing rate."

**Key Point**: Emphasize quality over quantity - these aren't random accounts but strategic additions

---

### Slide 4: Automation Tool
**Say**: "My biggest impact came from automating our roster management. Previously, updating rosters for NFL, NBA, and college sports took 2 months of manual work. I built a tool that reduces this to just 5 minutes of processing, plus about 30 minutes for free agency updates. That's a 99.5% time reduction, freeing our team to focus on more strategic work."

**Emphasize**: 
- Scale: 6,000+ roster entries
- Clean data organization with league-specific sheets
- Time savings translate to cost savings

---

### Slide 5: BR App UX
**Say**: "On the product side, I've been working on redesigning key user flows in the BR app. Through user research and iterative design, we've identified and addressed pain points in navigation and content discovery. [Mention 1-2 specific improvements based on your screenshots]"

**Note**: If you have the screenshots, this is where you'd show the before/after

---

### Slide 6: Results & Impact
**Say**: "Looking at the numbers, we've achieved a 733% increase in content sourcing rate and 240x faster roster processing. But beyond the metrics, we've built scalable systems that will continue delivering value long after my internship ends."

**Delivery**: Pause after big numbers for impact

---

### Slide 7: Key Learnings
**Say**: "This experience taught me three crucial lessons: First, investing time in automation creates exponential returns. Second, strategic content curation beats volume every time. And third, truly understanding user needs is the foundation of great product design."

**Tip**: These learnings show maturity and growth mindset

---

### Slide 8: Thank You
**Say**: "Thank you for this opportunity to contribute to Bleacher Report. I'm happy to answer any questions."

**Body Language**: Stand confidently, smile, be ready for questions

---

## Q&A Preparation

### Likely Questions & Answers:

**Q: How did you identify which Twitter sources to add?**
A: "I analyzed engagement metrics, looked for gaps in our coverage (like local markets), and capitalized on trends like the podcast boom. Each source was evaluated for authenticity, engagement rate, and content quality."

**Q: What technology did you use for the automation tool?**
A: "I used [mention specific technologies - Python, Excel VBA, APIs, etc.]. The key was creating a solution that non-technical team members could easily use and maintain."

**Q: How do you measure the success of the UX improvements?**
A: "We're tracking metrics like user engagement time, navigation patterns, and user feedback. Early indicators show [mention any positive metrics if available]."

**Q: What's next for these projects?**
A: "The automation tool can be expanded to other leagues, the sourcing strategy can be templated for other content types, and the UX improvements provide a framework for future app updates."

---

## Presentation Tips

1. **Energy**: Start strong and maintain enthusiasm
2. **Eye Contact**: Rotate around the room, don't just look at one person
3. **Pacing**: Don't rush - you have enough time
4. **Stories**: The LeBron podcast example is relatable - use it
5. **Confidence**: You've done impressive work - own it

## Technical Setup
- Have backup slides on USB/cloud
- Test equipment 15 minutes early
- Bring water
- Have phone on silent
